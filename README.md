# Cinnabar Lab - Support

Cinnabar Lab is your ultimate Pokémon companion app for iOS.

## Features

- **Game Selector** - 22 supported games; the whole app adapts to your choice
- **Complete Pokédex** - All 1025 Pokémon plus alternate forms, with stats, abilities, evolution chains, learnsets, catch locations, and type matchups
- **Mega Dex** - Mega Evolutions with mega-specific types, sprites, and stone locations
- **Database** - Built-in reference data on a fast SQLite layer
- **Type Chart & Candy Calculator** - Quick reference and candy planning
- **Pokémon Quiz** - Test your knowledge across all generations
- **Living Dex Tracker** - Track your collection progress with export/import
- **Team Builder** - Type coverage analysis and an optimizer that fills your gaps
- **Completely Offline** - No internet required
